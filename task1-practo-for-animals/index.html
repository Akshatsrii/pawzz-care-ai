Emergency First-Aid Quick Protocols

🚨 Suspected Poisoning (Chocolate, Rat Poison, Lilies)

Do NOT induce vomiting without consulting a vet. Take a photo of the toxin container and immediately drive to 24/7 ICU.

🩹 Bleeding & Vehicular Trauma

Apply direct, firm pressure with a clean cloth. Keep the animal warm and still to avoid shock. Do not twist the spine during transit.

☀️ Heatstroke & Panting Distress

Move into air conditioning immediately. Wet paw pads and belly with room-temperature water (never ice water). Offer small sips of water.
