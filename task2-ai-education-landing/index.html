Live cohort workshops take place on Saturdays and Sundays (6:00 PM – 8:00 PM IST). All sessions are recorded in high-definition and uploaded within 2 hours. Expect to spend approximately 4–5 hours per week building your hands-on project.

No! We provide shared sandbox API credits and guide you through generous free tiers for Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, Supabase, Groq, and self-hosted n8n instances, keeping your out-of-pocket costs at virtually ₹0.
